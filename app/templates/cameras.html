    Camera controls and profiles

    Controls apply to the selected camera while Autodarts runs. Switch automatic modes off and apply before adjusting dependent controls. Drivers may refuse changes during capture. Profiles are saved per camera and applied on request.

    Select a camera to read its controls.

    Selected camera

    Exposure and gain calibration

    1. Set up your usual lighting and focus the lens. Select a detailed area containing board wire and, if useful, a stationary dart.
    2. Start a sweep for the selected camera. Keep the board, lighting and lens still. Detection may be less reliable while exposure changes.
    3. Review the measured scores. Previous controls are restored after the sweep; apply the proposal only if you want to keep it.

    The sweep compares detail and clipping, with exposure tested at low gain before trying higher gain. It does not replace Autodarts board calibration. A higher image score is not a guarantee of better dart detection.
